# Cookie policy

This website **does not use first-party or third-party cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking**, so we do not ask for your consent.

Visit statistics are collected with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not identify visitors.

On the contact page, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent spam. It is a technical system needed to send the form and is not used for tracking.

If we ever add cookies that require your consent, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
