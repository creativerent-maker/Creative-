# Accessibility statement

Creative Rent is committed to making this website accessible, aiming to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1.

## Measures taken
- Sufficient colour contrast between text and background.
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus.
- Alternative text for images.
- Responsive layout for any screen size, with zoom allowed.
- Animations stop if the device has reduced motion enabled.
